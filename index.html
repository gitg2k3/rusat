About Rusat

Packaging Engineered Around What You Need to Protect

Established in 1990, Rusat Timber Depot is an experienced manufacturer of wooden packaging solutions for industrial, manufacturing, logistics, and export applications. Founded with a dedication to structural integrity and precision craftsmanship, our mission is to deliver custom timber packaging that combines heavy-duty durability, optimal protection, and seamless compliance.
